## How the topics were picked

1. **Your own numbers first.** Your most-watched videos are sugar (59,645 views), a virus
   getting in (25,039), the brain cleaning itself (4,084), wounds healing (2,811), asthma (2,394),
   insulin (1,975) and white blood cells (1,817). Those themes come back as Toons, early. They are
   marked ★ below.
2. **Things everybody has done or wondered.** Swallowed gum, held a pee, popped a pimple,
   cracked knuckles, had brain freeze. The viewer already wants the answer before the video starts.
3. **One body scene unlocks many videos.** A new scene (skin, brain, lungs) is the big piece of
   drawing. So each new scene arrives on a set day and is then used again and again.
4. **Three different body parts a day** where possible, so a viewer who sees two of your videos
   in a row does not see the same picture twice.

**"Viral" I cannot promise, and nobody honestly can.** What this plan does is raise the odds:
106 tries in a month, on topics people already search for, led by the themes your channel has
already proven, and changed every week by the real watch numbers.

**"High end" I can hold.** Every video passes the same five measured checks and a listen before
you see it. A video that fails does not go out that day. Pace never beats quality.

---

## How to read the calendar

- Video **1** each day is the lead: the strongest topic, posted at the best time.
- **4th** is the day's easiest video (its scene already exists, little or nothing new to draw).
  It is the first to drop if a day runs long.
- After each title: the body scene, then where the facts will come from.
- ★ = a theme already proven on your channel. ⚠ = needs extra care (list at the end).
- Three hook choices for every video come with that day's script sheet, as before.

## How a day runs (two yeses from you, not ten)

1. **The sheet.** One page with the day's 3 or 4 scripts: title, three hooks (my pick marked),
   storyboard, sources, and the voice cost for the whole batch.
   **Your yes no. 1** covers those scripts and that voice cost, nothing more.
2. I voice, render, listen, and run every check. If a video fails, I tell you which and why,
   and it moves to the next day.
3. **The batch.** The finished videos, to you.
   **Your yes no. 2** covers uploading exactly those videos, set to go public at that day's
   times, not made for kids, the AI-use question answered Yes, no caption track, and each
   video's question comment once it is public.

Until now every step had its own yes. Two a day is a change, so it needs your approval.
Anything outside the day's sheet still gets its own question.

**Posting times to start with (India time):** 8 am, 1 pm, 6 pm, and 9 pm for a 4th.
These are a starting guess. I move them when your channel's numbers show when your viewers
are actually watching.

**Every Tuesday:** the watch numbers for the week (they arrive 1 to 3 days late). Topics,
hooks and scenes that hold viewers get more days; ones that lose viewers get swapped.

## Topics that need extra care (⚠)

- **Swallow gum:** I have no source yet that tests the "seven years" story. The script says
  only what the sources say.
- **Tea too hot:** the WHO agency's word is "probably". The script uses exactly that.
- **Knocked-out tooth, choking, burn, stroke, heart attack:** the steps and warning signs are
  taken exactly from the NHS or CDC page, never from memory, and never made frightening.
- **Hold in a sneeze, too much water:** real harm is rare. The script says rare.
- **Hold in a fart:** the published research is thin. This one may be swapped.
- **Butterflies, hangry, brain freeze, jerking awake:** the cause is partly theory. The script
  says "scientists think".
- **Tattoo ink:** the main finding is from a study in mice. The script says so.
- **Sugar and your brain:** no "addiction" claim.

Only the first week's seven original topics have been researched so far (gum, night brushing,
growling, lemon water, hot tea, grinding, burping; details in `week-1-2026-11-18.md`).
Every other topic is a candidate until its sources are read, the day before its sheet.
