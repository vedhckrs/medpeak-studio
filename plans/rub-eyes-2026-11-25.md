## The idea in one line

Inside Kid's itchy eye, a pair of giant cheeky Knuckles press down on the eye and rub, rub, rub; germs hop off them onto
the eye. Months of hard rubbing later the eye's clear front dome has gone thin and pointy. At the clinic the doctor
explains: rubbing hard and often can thin the front of the eye and change its shape; a cold flannel and clean hands
instead, and when to get help fast. Back in the eye, the Knuckles give up and leave.

- **The opening (O1-O5):** frame 1 is the WHOLE EYE in a side cut-away filling the frame, its face on the white of the eye,
  the clear dome (the cornea) at the front, already red and itchy; the Knuckles press down on the lid from above. One place
  until the time card (about 16 s), two characters (the Eye and the Knuckles; the germs come at 8-10 s, small).
- **Inside the body about 65%.** People are only voices until the clinic.
- **The twist is a picture:** after the card the same framing shows the dome thinner and bulging into a point.

## The facts (allowed sources only, words read on 2026-11-25)

| # | Fact in the video | Source (exact words) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (story) | Hands carry germs to the eyes | NEI (NIH), pink eye: "Always wash your hands before touching your eyes."; "Viral and bacterial pink eye spread very easily from person to person" |
| 2 (answer) | Rubbing hard and for long can thin the front of the eye and change its shape (keratoconus) | PubMed 31343437 (review, 2019): "Vigorous and prolonged rubbing may lead to establishment of corneal remodeling and ectatic disorders, such as keratoconus." "...mechanical mechanisms of corneal thinning and its loss of rigidity..." PubMed 19512912 (review, 2009): "There appear to be indications for the control of chronic habits of abnormal rubbing." |
| 3 (answer) | Instead: a cold flannel; do not rub; wash hands before touching the eyes | NHS, conjunctivitis: "Hold a cold flannel on your eyes for a few minutes to cool them down."; "do not rub your eyes"; NEI: "Always wash your hands before touching your eyes." |
| care | Get urgent help for eye pain, light sensitivity, vision changes or very red eyes | NHS, conjunctivitis (urgent GP / 111): "pain in your eyes", "sensitivity to light", "changes in your vision, like wavy lines or flashing", "very red eyes" |

Sources: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31343437, pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19512912, nhs.uk/conditions/conjunctivitis/,
nei.nih.gov pink eye page, nhs.uk/conditions/eye-injuries/ (for the description's A&E list).

**Care in the wording:** the reviews say "may" and "vigorous and prolonged"; the video says "rubbing HARD and OFTEN can",
never that one rub harms the eye. The card says "MONTHS LATER", not a number. No "blurry vision" line (no allowed source read
for it). The national NHS and NEI keratoconus pages were not reachable (moved); the reviews carry fact 2.
