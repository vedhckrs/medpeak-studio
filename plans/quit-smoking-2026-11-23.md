## The idea in one line

Dad snaps his last cigarette. Inside his chest, the grey, tarry lungs can't believe it, the sleepy cilia crew wakes up, and
the carbon monoxide gang riding his red blood cells gets kicked out. Two "LATER" cards show the same pictures healing. Then the
twist: the lungs cough MORE, and Dr. Mira explains why that is good news.

- **Format 6, shape `visit`**: one story, one condition, 2 body places (the chest, inside a blood vessel), 2 time cards, then
  Dr. Mira's answer. About 55 s of story, about 4 cuts a minute, inside the body about 65%.
- **The new engine is used throughout**: the chest and vessel stages, the cilia crew (MiniWorker cleaners), the CO eggs,
  the hero cigarette, the shared dive, the time cards, face-only talking, real pauses, the funk bed, the 5.5 s end card.
