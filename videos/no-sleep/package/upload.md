## Description

```
What happens if you don't sleep? While you sleep, the spaces between brain cells open up and fluid washes waste away (shown in mice). After one night without sleep, brain scans of healthy people showed more beta-amyloid, a waste protein. After 17 to 19 hours awake, people did some tests as badly as at a blood alcohol level of 0.05%. Adults need 7 or more hours of sleep, says the CDC.

Sources:
• Xie L et al. Sleep drives metabolite clearance from the adult brain. Science 2013;342:373-7: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24136970/

• Williamson AM, Feyer AM. Moderate sleep deprivation produces impairments ... equivalent to legally prescribed levels of alcohol intoxication. Occup Environ Med 2000;57:649-55: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10984335/
```
