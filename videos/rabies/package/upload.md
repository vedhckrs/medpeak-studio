What happens if you get rabies? The virus enters through a bite or scratch, usually from a dog, and travels slowly along a nerve toward the brain. Once symptoms start, rabies is almost always fatal, but treatment given in time stops the virus before it gets there. The World Health Organization says: wash the wound with soap and water for at least 15 minutes straight away, then see a doctor the same day.
