## Sources

- **who-rabies** World Health Organization. Rabies, fact sheet (17 September 2026). 'Dog bites and scratches cause 99% of the human rabies cases.' Rabies spreads 'via saliva, usually through bites, scratches, or direct contact with mucosa'. 'The incubation period for rabies is typically 2–3 months but may vary from one week to one year'. 'Once clinical symptoms appear, rabies is virtually 100% fatal.' 'Rabies deaths are preventable with prompt post exposure prophylaxis (PEP) by stopping the virus from reaching the central nervous system.' 'If a person is bitten or scratched by a potentially rabid animal, they should immediately and always wash the wound with water and soap for at least 15 minutes and seek medical attention.' PEP is wound washing, 'a course of human rabies vaccine and, when indicated, rabies immunoglobulins'. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/rabies>
- **cdc-rabies** US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. About Rabies (27 January 2026). 'Rabies spreads primarily through bites or scratches from an infected animal.' 'The rabies virus must travel to the brain before it can cause symptoms.' Treatment after exposure is 'nearly 100% effective if you get it after exposure'. 'Once clinical signs of rabies appear, the disease is nearly always fatal.' <https://www.cdc.gov/rabies/about/index.html>
