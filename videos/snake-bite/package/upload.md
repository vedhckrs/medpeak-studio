## Description

```
What happens when a snake bites you? Venom from a venomous snake can cause paralysis that can stop breathing, bleeding problems, kidney failure and tissue damage (WHO). What to do while you wait for help (NHS): stay calm, keep the bitten part of your body as still as you can, take off rings and other jewellery in case it swells, and call your emergency number. Do NOT cut or suck the bite, do NOT tie anything tightly around it, and do not go near the snake. At the hospital, antivenom is the most effective treatment. Some bites from venomous snakes carry no venom at all, but you cannot tell, so get every snake bite checked. Worldwide, about 5.4 million people are bitten by snakes every year (WHO).

Sources:
```
