## Description

What happens to your body when you fast for 16 hours? Hunger comes in waves at your usual meal times and drops again on its own. After about 12 hours or more without food, the liver's sugar store runs low and the body switches to burning fat. But in a year-long trial, fasting every other day lost about the same weight as simply eating less every day, and eating in an 8-hour window did no better either. Fasting isn't for everyone: researchers advise against it in pregnancy and for anyone with a history of eating disorders. Ask a doctor first.
