What happens when you quit smoking? Coughing more at first is usually a good sign: your lungs are clearing out the mucus and toxins built up from smoking, and your airways are cleaning themselves (NHS). After 8 hours the carbon monoxide in your blood halves and your oxygen levels recover; after 72 hours breathing can feel easier because your bronchial tubes start to relax (NHS). Getting support can really increase your chances of quitting for good (NHS). See a doctor if a cough lasts more than 3 weeks, and get urgent help if you cough up blood or find it hard to breathe (NHS).
