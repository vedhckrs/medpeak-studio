## Description

```
What happens when a bee stings you? The sting usually leaves a small mark, and sometimes the stinger stays stuck in the skin (NHS). Brush or scrape it out sideways with a fingernail or the edge of a bank card; do not use tweezers, as you could squeeze poison out of it (NHS, CDC). Wash the area with soap and water, hold an ice pack wrapped in a cloth on it for up to 20 minutes, and keep it raised if you can (NHS). A small swollen lump is normal and should get better in a few days (NHS). Call your emergency number (999 in the UK, 911 in the US, 112 in the EU) at once if the lips, mouth, throat or tongue suddenly swell, or someone is struggling to swallow or breathe: these can be signs of a serious allergic reaction (NHS).
```
