## Sources

- **nhs-heartburn** NHS. Heartburn and acid reflux (page last reviewed 20 November 2023). 'Heartburn is a burning feeling in the chest caused by stomach acid travelling up towards the throat (acid reflux).' 'Symptoms are often worse after eating, when lying down and when bending over.' Advice: 'eat smaller, more frequent meals', 'do not eat within 3 or 4 hours before bed', 'raise the head of your bed by around 10 to 20cm'. <https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heartburn-and-acid-reflux/>
- **niddk-gerd** NIH National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases. Symptoms and Causes of GER and GERD (reviewed July 2020). 'GERD may develop if your lower esophageal sphincter becomes weak or relaxes when it shouldn't.' The lower esophageal sphincter is the ring of muscle between the food pipe and the stomach. <https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/acid-reflux-ger-gerd-adults/symptoms-causes>
