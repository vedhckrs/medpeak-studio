What happens if you lie down after eating a big dinner? Your stomach is full, the valve at its top can relax, and acid flows up your food pipe: that burning in your chest is heartburn. This cartoon shows it from inside the stomach, and Dr. Mira shares the NHS advice: no food for three or four hours before bed.
