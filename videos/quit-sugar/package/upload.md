## Description

```
What happens if you quit sugar for 30 days? Little evidence says sugar is addictive in people, so the cravings are not a drug withdrawal. Juice, honey and syrups still count as free sugar; the WHO's limit is under 10% of daily energy, about 12 teaspoons for someone eating about 2,000 calories, and caries are lower below it. In a 9-day study of children with obesity, swapping sugar for starch at the same calories nearly halved liver fat (7.2% to 3.8%). The WHO advises not to swap sugar for sweeteners. In trials of adults, eating less sugar took off about 0.8 kg, because people ate fewer calories. And in one small trial, after three months on less sugar, sweet foods tasted about 40% sweeter.
```
