## Description

```
What happens if you skip brushing at night? Mouth germs turn leftover food into acid, and while you sleep your spit, which clears sugar and acid by day, almost stops flowing. This cartoon shows the night from the top of a tooth: the spit guards fall asleep and the germs throw a party. The fix is the NHS advice: brush last thing before bed for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste, then spit and do not rinse.
```
