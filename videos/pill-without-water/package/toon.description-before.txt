What happens when you swallow a pill without water? It can stick to the wall of your food pipe, dissolve there and irritate it, which is why your chest can burn when you swallow. This cartoon shows it from inside the food pipe, and Dr. Mira explains the simple fix: a glass of water, sitting or standing up.
