What sugar does to your teeth: mouth bacteria turn it into acid, and the acid attacks your enamel until a cavity forms. This cartoon shows it on a tooth cut open. Then Dr. Mira explains the twist: your own spit helps repair enamel, if you snack less often and brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste.
