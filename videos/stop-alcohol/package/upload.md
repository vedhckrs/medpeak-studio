## Description

```
What happens when you stop drinking alcohol? Drinking can disrupt your sleep and stop you sleeping deeply, so cutting down should help you feel more rested when you wake up (NHS). The NHS lists other quick changes: feeling better in the mornings, being less tired during the day, better-looking skin, feeling more energetic and better weight management. If you stop drinking, your liver can often recover (NHS). But if your body is dependent on alcohol, it can be very dangerous to stop suddenly: get medical help before you stop or cut down (NHS). Withdrawal symptoms usually begin within 6 to 12 hours of the last drink (NHS). Call your emergency number (999 in the UK, 911 in the US, 112 in the EU) if someone has severe withdrawal symptoms after stopping, such as visible shaking, confusion, seeing or hearing things that are not there, or a seizure (NHS). If you are worried about your drinking, talk to a doctor or a local alcohol support service.
```
